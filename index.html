Our Story

Helping the World Achieve Carbon Neutrality

Founded by Okoye Bonny Chukwurah, Chybonsolar Energy started in The Gambia in 2000 and expanded into Nigeria in 2008. We hold a leadership position in renewable energy across Sub-Saharan Africa.

Our mission is to contribute significantly towards meeting basic energy needs by offering reliable, sustainable solutions — making a positive impact on the environment and communities we serve.
